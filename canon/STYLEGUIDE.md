# Super Intelligent Hacker: style guide

## The words

Plain, calm and on your side: the hacker who helps you shut each door, never one who scares you. Facts only from the owner.

## The look

Deep green ground, cream ink and an amber accent, Bangers headings over Comic Neue text, the family comic panels.

## The pictures

The owner's own pictures, each with alt text that says what is in it. Any other rule is the owner's to set.
